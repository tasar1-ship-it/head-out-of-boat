The big idea

You're the driver.
Be the drone too.

The Driver
Down low in the boat. You feel the speed and steer — but you only see what's right next to you.
The Drone
High above the course. You see the wind, the fleet, the mark — the whole game at once.

The best sailors are both at the same time.

Head down in the boat, you never see the stronger wind — so the sailor who looks up finds it and pulls away. Your boat was fine; you just never looked. Drive like a drone: get your head up, see where the better wind is, and go there.

The fleet splits

Right after the start the fleet splits left and right. When most boats commit to one side, that's a clue it may be paying — but read the wind yourself before you follow the crowd.

The boats change the wind too

Find clean air

WIND

A pack of boats leaves a big patch of messy, slow wind behind it. Stuck in that dirty air? Steer out to the side and follow the path into clean air — then speed away.

↓ One more thing
Your on-the-water checklist

Do this every lap

1Sail fast — trim, balance, steer well first.
2Every ~15s, look up — a quick 2–3 second scan.
3Ask: where's the better wind? Where's the fleet going?
4Decide — then get your head back in and sail fast again.

🛰️ Be the drone. Then be the driver. 🚤
